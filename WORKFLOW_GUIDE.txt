=============================

LOGIN FLOW
----------
1. Open the app and go to the login page.
2. Enter the demo email and password.
3. The backend trims whitespace and validates the normalized email before login.
4. On success, a JWT token is generated and stored locally.
5. The role-based dashboard is loaded from the logged-in user role.

CONSUMER FLOW
-------------
1. Login as consumer@farmdirect.ai.
2. Open the marketplace and browse listed products.
3. Add a product to cart.
4. The cart keeps the product quantity and the server-side price.
5. Place the order from the cart checkout flow.
6. The order is created with the price calculated by the backend.
7. Product quantity is reduced in the inventory after checkout.
8. Payment is made through the payment route for that order.
9. Order status moves through the lifecycle and becomes visible in orders history.

FARMER FLOW
-----------
1. Login as farmer@farmdirect.ai.
2. View the orders assigned to the farmer.
3. Review the buyer order and confirm the order when the payment is complete.
4. Confirmed orders can move to logistics request.
5. The farmer can trigger logistics handoff after confirming dispatch.
6. The system creates or updates delivery records tied to the order.

BULK BUYER FLOW
---------------
1. Login as buyer@farmdirect.ai.
2. Open the bulk buyer dashboard and view procurement requirements.
3. Create or review bulk requirements.
4. Use supplier matching and marketplace data to identify supply opportunities.
5. Place bulk order requirements through the role-specific workflow.
6. The procurement flow works with the same product, order, and payment logic used across the platform.

FPO FLOW
--------
1. Login as fpo@farmdirect.ai.
2. Review member farmers and aggregation records.
3. Add or inspect FPO-ledger information for grouped produce.
4. Use the FPO dashboard to track collective supply and member participation.

LOGISTICS FLOW
--------------
1. Login as logistics@farmdirect.ai.
2. Open the logistics dashboard and review the delivery queue.
3. Accept the assigned delivery.
4. Delivery status moves from AVAILABLE to ACCEPTED, then through vehicle/driver assignment and movement events.
5. Tracking events reflect the delivery journey until completion.
6. The route estimate screen helps the logistics team plan pickup and destination movement.

ADMIN FLOW
----------
1. Login as admin@farmdirect.ai.
2. Open the admin dashboard to review system health.
3. View platform overview values like users, farmers, orders, and revenue.
4. Review analytics by order status and user role.
5. Manage users and activate or deactivate accounts.
6. Review all products in the catalogue.
7. Inspect all orders and their transaction status.
8. Review audit logs for platform actions and changes.
